Currently using the following fields:
'rgdpe': Real GDP measured using the expenditure approach, at constant prices and purchasing power parity (PPP).
'emp': Total number of people employed in the economy (in millions).
'ck': Capital stock at constant prices
'csh_i': Investment share of GDP
'rtfpna': Real total factor productivity (TFP) {captures efficiency over technology}
'csh_c': Share of household consumption in GDP

With the following modifications:
pct_change (over 1, 2, 3, 5, 10 years): How much the value has changed from ... years ago
acceleration: The change in percentage change between consecutive periods
zscore (over 3, 5 & 10 years)
vs_global: the value compared to the global mean
percentile: percentile rank within each year
lag (1, 2, 3 years): values from the past (1, 2 or 3) year(s)

When year is added to training, results will be slightly better

Using Developed & Developing countries: The results are slightly better for both
But when adding Least Developed: Results are worse
